# Featured Selection Rules

Featured is an Owner-facing editorial decision over adopted, identity-verified papers. A machine may prepare a shortlist but must not silently promote it.

Evaluate the set, not papers in isolation:

- research-direction coverage and complementarity;
- current relevance and historical trajectory;
- author role where independently supported;
- student interpretability;
- recency without novelty bias.

Newest, most cited, official-list, first-author or last-author status is never an automatic featured rule. Historical adopted papers may belong in trajectory rather than the current featured set. Record Owner decisions and keep global human/release gates separate.
