# AI Synthesis Boundaries

Every rendered statement belongs to one visible lane:

- `PUBLIC FACT`: directly supported by official public sources.
- `PUBLICATION-SUPPORTED`: supported by adopted identity-verified papers.
- `AI SYNTHESIS`: conditional cross-source organization or explanation.
- `UNRESOLVED`: plausible but not closed; never rendered as fact.

Do not infer current projects, recruitment, quota, lab atmosphere, mentoring style, efficacy, outcomes, project availability or student autonomy.

An adopted historical paper does not establish current research priority. Hao Jiang's adopted set supports tumor mechanisms more strongly than a tumor–nerve trajectory; keep that limitation explicit.

For undergraduate tasks, prefer: “公开资料不足以确认本科生具体任务，建议向导师询问本科生通常从哪些实验、数据分析或文献任务开始。” Do not turn an Academic Intelligence evidence-review exercise into a claimed lab assignment.
