# Author Identity Verification

## Identity seed

Capture Chinese/English names, pinyin and initials, current and historical units, official email, public ORCID candidates, topics, methods, coauthors and official profile URL. A seed starts discovery; it is not a conclusion.

## Per-record closure

For every paper record positive evidence and counterevidence across:

- exact name form and author position;
- publication-time department/institution;
- official or publication email;
- ORCID consistency and provenance;
- topic and long-term coauthor network;
- official publication-list linkage and plausible timeline.

Name + university, topic fit, or an OpenAlex entity is insufficient alone. Broad PubMed name/affiliation searches produce false positives and require record-level review.

One ORCID-bearing publication does not automatically verify advisor-level ORCID. Keep the Fan Liangliang/Hao Jiang pattern unresolved absent stronger independent closure. If plausible papers expose conflicting ORCIDs, retain both and select neither, as with Jinchen Li. Per-paper identity may still close by other evidence; advisor ORCID is a separate claim.

An exact title on an official list is strong evidence, but if the contract also requires publication-author closure, keep the paper unresolved until that record closes. Hao Jiang E5 is the reference case.
